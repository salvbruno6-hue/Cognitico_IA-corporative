# ELO-FORGE — Specialist Skill Registry

## Canonical ownership

The Specialist Skill Registry is the canonical Forge mechanism for representing domain specialists and governed skills.

- **ELO Cognitivo**: normative authority, identity, invariants, governance and promotion rules.
- **Core**: shared cognitive faculty and generalized validated mechanisms.
- **Forge**: specialist technique, contextual knowledge, procedures, parameters, experience, testing and learning candidates.
- **Application / Infrastructure**: replaceable means.

A specialist is a governed technical capability. It is not a second intelligence, Core, memory authority, supervisor or orchestrator.

## Mandatory lifecycle

`SOURCE → EVIDENCE → FORGE SKILL → TEST → EMPIRICAL VALIDATION → CONTEXTUAL EXPERIENCE → GENERALIZATION → EVOLUTION GATE → OPTIONAL CORE PROMOTION`

The originating Forge experience remains preserved when a generalized learning candidate is promoted.

## Skill contract

Every registered skill must explicitly declare:

1. identity and domain family;
2. purpose and scope;
3. technical boundaries and prohibited authority;
4. prerequisites and authorized inputs;
5. authorized sources and provenance requirements;
6. procedures and calculation methods;
7. evidence, fact/commitment/availability/assumption/estimate/hypothesis/GAP/conflict handling;
8. uncertainty, risk and escalation rules;
9. outputs and decision boundaries;
10. executable test references and maturity;
11. version and historical lineage;
12. contextual parameters and company overlays;
13. learning candidates;
14. candidates for Core promotion.

## Initial domain families

The registry provides a common representation for HR, Budgeting, PCP, Procurement, Logistics, Commercial, Finance, Engineering, Quality, Risk, Corporate Process Analysis and Data Analysis/Calculation/Simulation.

The presence of a domain family does **not** mean that its specialist is implemented or empirically validated. Maturity must be explicit.

## External sources

Courses, providers, plugins, libraries, documents and external tools may supply evidence or capability resources. They do not become canonical authority merely by being registered.

External material must pass authorization, provenance, compatibility, security and validation controls before being incorporated into a Forge skill.

## Promotion boundary

Forge may propose learning candidates. It may not directly mutate Core. Promotion requires sufficient validation, generalization and the existing Evolution Gate.

## Non-negotiables

- one canonical specialist registry;
- no duplicate specialist authority;
- no domain-specific Core;
- no direct Forge → Core promotion;
- provenance and authorization are mandatory;
- missing information remains GAP rather than invented data;
- historical experience is preserved;
- contextual company overlays remain scoped;
- skill maturity is evidence-based.
