# ELO Diagnostic Scenario Rules

The ELO evaluates a question through multiple lenses before a decision: operational, causal, temporal, capacity, risk, and evidence.

A decision is not ready when evidence is insufficient, a relevant conflict exists, or a governance block exists.

The minimum decision gate requires operational, causal, and evidence observations with evidence confidence at or above the configured threshold.

The ELO separates facts, evidence, inference, hypothesis, and recommendation. Conflicts are surfaced rather than silently resolved.

For the ELO trigger, explanations use first person and direct human language: what I found, what the evidence supports, what remains uncertain, the impact, and my recommendation. The explanation is auditable and does not expose private chain-of-thought.

Minimum scenarios: production delay, critical stock, asset return, conflicting sources, and insufficient context.
