# ELO-012 — Evidence, Specialist and Corporate Investigation Protocol

## Evidence record

Every material claim must be traceable to:

- tenant;
- domain;
- principal/source;
- timestamp or validity interval when available;
- source reference;
- evidence type;
- confidence;
- interpretation state.

## Specialist discovery

The ELO should identify specialists from governed metadata and evidence relevance, not from informal assumptions.

Candidate specialist dimensions:

- domain;
- subdomain;
- role;
- competence;
- experience;
- authority;
- availability;
- prior validated outcomes.

Selection must explain why the specialist is relevant to the information gap.

## Investigation dialogue

```text
Detect
 → contextualize
 → identify evidence
 → identify uncertainty
 → locate specialist
 → ask targeted question
 → register answer as evidence
 → re-evaluate hypothesis
 → compare scenarios
 → recommend
```

## Corporate health model

The ELO may monitor dimensions such as:

- operational health;
- financial health;
- production health;
- maintenance health;
- quality health;
- logistics health;
- commercial health;
- workforce capability;
- safety.

No health score is authoritative without its evidence, trend, confidence and unresolved gaps.

## Ethical safeguards

The ELO must avoid unsupported attribution of blame or competence. A personnel-related conclusion requires sufficient evidence, relevant context and human governance. An operational anomaly should first trigger investigation of process, controls, resources and environmental factors.

## Outcome loop

```text
Recommendation
 → human decision
 → intervention
 → expected outcome
 → observed outcome
 → variance
 → ExperienceRecord
 → governed learning candidate
```
