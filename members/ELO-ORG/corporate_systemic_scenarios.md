# ELO-ORG — Corporate Systemic Scenario Pack

## Purpose

Define the minimum executable scenarios for cross-domain enterprise analysis. These scenarios consume the canonical domain model; they do not create a parallel organizational catalog.

## Scenarios

1. Comercial → Orçamento
2. Comercial → Licitações → Orçamento
3. Orçamento → Projetos/Engenharia → Produção
4. PCP → Produção → Logística
5. Comercial → Orçamento → PCP → Produção
6. Missing or conflicting cross-domain relation
7. Same-domain flow variation compared with existing faculty
8. Member detachment while promoted knowledge remains
9. New domain mechanic evaluated for promotion/overlay/alternative
10. Executive multidomain view of risks, dependencies and impacts

## Required result semantics

- `SUPPORTED`: relations are represented and supported by evidence.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: required relation/evidence is missing.
- `CONFLICTING`: material evidence or structural assertions disagree.
- `BLOCKED`: authorization, tenant, principal or execution context prevents safe analysis.

No missing relation may be interpreted as proof that a relationship does not exist.
