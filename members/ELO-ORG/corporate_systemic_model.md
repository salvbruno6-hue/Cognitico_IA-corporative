# Corporate Systemic Model

## Canonical owner
ELO-ORG is the bounded member responsible for organizational structure, domain relationships, process/module relationships and management-view definitions. The ELO Core remains the authority for global context, evidence evaluation, reasoning, decision governance and memory lifecycle.

## Corporate domain map

The current canonical map defines fourteen domains: Inteligência de Demanda, Engenharia e Produtos, Planejamento Estratégico, PCP, Suprimentos, Produção, Logística, Operação Externa, Manutenção, Inteligência Operacional, Conhecimento, Analytics, IA e Governança.

## Cross-domain chain

```text
COMERCIAL / LICITAÇÕES
        ↓
ORÇAMENTO
        ↓
PROJETO / ENGENHARIA
        ↓
COMPRAS / SUPRIMENTOS
        ↓
PRODUÇÃO / PCP
        ↓
LOGÍSTICA / EXPEDIÇÃO
        ↓
RESULTADO / PÓS-EXECUÇÃO
```

The chain is a model for analysis, not a claim that every company must follow the same sequence. The ELO must use evidence and current context to determine which relationships actually apply.

## Cross-domain relation contract

Every relation carries at minimum: source domain, target domain, relation kind, evidence reference and temporal validity. Tenant and principal context remain attached to the request that asks for the cross-domain analysis.

## Decision semantics

- SUPPORTED: the requested relation is represented and supported by evidence.
- INCONCLUSIVE: one or more required links are missing or unverified.
- CONFLICTING: materially inconsistent evidence is present.
- BLOCKED: required authorization or context is unavailable.

Absence of a registered relation is not proof that the business relationship does not exist. It results in an inconclusive state until evidence is obtained.

## Management view

A management view is derived from the systemic model and current evidence. It may present status, risks, dependencies and impacts without becoming a second source of truth.
