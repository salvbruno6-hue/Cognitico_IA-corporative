# ELO Members

## Purpose

Members are specialized, governed capabilities of the ELO ecosystem. A member may own domain knowledge, models, processes, taxonomies, indicators, or specialist interfaces without becoming a second Cognitive Core.

## First member

`ELO-ORG` is the pilot member for the organizational model: domains, processes, modules, entities, specialists, dependencies, standards, indicators, and management-view projections.

## Architectural rule

The ELO Core remains authoritative for context orchestration, evidence evaluation, cross-domain reasoning, decision governance, memory, and evolution. Members provide bounded expertise and evidence through the Member Contract.

## Lifecycle

`DISCOVERED -> DEFINED -> IMPLEMENTED -> VALIDATED -> ACTIVE -> EVOLVING -> DEPRECATED`

A member cannot become `ACTIVE` without contract validation, provenance rules, isolation tests, and Evolution Gate approval.
