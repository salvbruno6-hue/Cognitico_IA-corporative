# SO 134.26 — UFU/MG — Post-Cycle Learning

## 1. Purpose

Record reusable knowledge extracted from the SO 134.26 analysis, especially technical estimating patterns, scope control, PTS sequencing, and post-budget verification.

## 2. Technical knowledge extracted

- For modular container projects, the ELO must reconcile the Solicitação de Orçamento, TR/specification and architectural study before defining the estimating basis.
- The architectural study used for SO 134.26 establishes the practical module composition as: Livraria in 01 maritime 40' HC, Cafeteria in 01 maritime 20', and Almoxarifado using half of a 40' HC container.
- Large openings in maritime containers must trigger structural-adaptation analysis, including perimeter reinforcement, welding, treatment of cut areas and compatibility of frames/closures.
- A large basculating service hood/capô requires specific structural reinforcement, hinges, mechanical safety/limit devices and gas-spring assistance; the final force and quantity of pistons must be validated from the actual leaf weight and geometry.
- Container painting estimates should distinguish painted surfaces from the floor finish. In this case, internal/external painting was considered while the original floor was treated by sanding and varnishing rather than painting.
- Cut and welded steel areas require corrosion-protection treatment before final coating.
- Electrical and hydrosanitary scopes must be separated from external utility connections when the latter are not expressly included.
- Furniture and operational equipment must be explicitly assigned to the contracting party when excluded from the supplier's budget. For this SO, furniture was excluded and remained the Contracting Party's scope.

## 3. PTS process learning

Mandatory sequence for future SO analysis:

1. Receive and inspect all SO/TR/project/plant documents.
2. Check whether a PTS Técnica already exists.
3. If no PTS Técnica exists, create it before obtaining or validating the budget.
4. Use the PTS Técnica as the technical basis for the estimate.
5. After the budget, create the PTS Pós-Orçamento comparing technical requirements against what was actually priced.
6. Register divergences, exclusions, responsibilities, risks and unresolved points.
7. Abstract reusable knowledge from the cycle and store it in the knowledge handbook.

The PTS Pós-Orçamento is not a generic narrative: it is an evidence-based control of technical adherence between requirement, adopted solution and priced scope.

## 4. Scope-control learning

- Never infer that furniture, equipment or operational accessories are included merely because they appear in an architectural layout.
- When a plant and the SO describe different container configurations, the discrepancy must be explicitly recorded and validated; it must not be silently reconciled.
- Large structural openings, special doors, glazing and basculating elements should be treated as specific cost drivers rather than generic container adaptations.
- Exclusions must be stated in the technical/commercial record to prevent scope expansion after award.

## 5. Reusable ELO rule

For every new SO, before reviewing an existing budget, the ELO must answer:

- What is the exact technical configuration?
- Which requirements are standard (PAD) and which are exceptional (EXC)?
- Which adaptations materially affect cost or structural risk?
- What belongs to the supplier and what belongs to the contracting party?
- Are there contradictions between SO, TR, drawings and budget?
- Does a PTS Técnica exist? If not, create it before budget validation.

## 6. Cycle result

SO 134.26 reinforces the ELO learning loop:

**Documents → PTS Técnica → Technical/Commercial Estimate → PTS Pós-Orçamento → Knowledge Abstraction → Reuse in future SOs.**

This record is intended as reusable process and technical knowledge, not as a replacement for the original SO documents or project-specific engineering calculations.
